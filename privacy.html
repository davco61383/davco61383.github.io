Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you visit Elementarium at davco61383.github.io (“the site”), an interactive periodic table made by David Shier.

No accounts, no personal data collected by the site

The site has no sign-up, no login, and no forms. It does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information, and it does not run its own server-side database of visitors.

Storage in your browser

To remember your choices, the app saves a few small settings in your own browser’s local storage, such as your chosen language, background, tile style, panel style, and whether spoken element names and music are on. It may also keep a short session flag and cache audio in your browser’s storage so it does not have to be fetched again. These values stay on your device, are not sent to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Advertising and cookies

The site uses Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick (DART) cookie, enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and how it uses cookies in advertising, at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons and IP addresses, to collect information as a result of ad serving on this site. Visitors in regions that require consent may be shown a consent message before personalized ads are used.

Other third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security and operations, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. Element photographs and descriptions may be loaded from Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia, which receive standard request information from your browser when those images load.

Children

The site is an educational reference and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page, which links to github.com/davco61383.
